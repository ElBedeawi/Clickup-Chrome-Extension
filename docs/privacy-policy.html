Privacy Policy

Video & Screenshot Uploader for ClickUp — published by Wagih Elbedeawi
Effective date: October 19, 2026

In short
  • Everything stays in your browser until you choose to send it to ClickUp.
  • Data goes to ClickUp only: its API (api.clickup.com), using your own token. The optional Clips tab (“Save to Clips”) also talks to ClickUp’s web-app hosts (id.app.clickup.com and your workspace’s *.clickup.com API host), using your existing ClickUp sign-in in this browser.
  • The developer runs no servers for this extension and receives none of your data.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no ads, and nothing is ever sold.

Who we are

Video & Screenshot Uploader for ClickUp (“the extension”) is a browser extension for Chrome and Firefox made by Wagih Elbedeawi (“the developer”, “we”). It is an independent product and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by ClickUp. “ClickUp” is a trademark of its owner and is used only to describe what the extension works with.

What the extension handles, and where it goes

DataWhyWhere it is kept / sent
Your ClickUp personal API token To create tasks and upload attachments on your behalf. Stored only in your browser (the extension's local storage). Sent only to api.clickup.com to authorize your requests.
Screen recordings (optionally with your camera picture as a bubble), microphone audio, screenshots and files you add To attach them to the ClickUp task you choose. Held in your browser (IndexedDB) until you upload or remove them, then sent to api.clickup.com when you press Create/Upload.
Task details you type (title, description, assignees, due date, tags, custom fields, comments) To create the task or post the comment. Sent to api.clickup.com when you submit. Not stored by the extension afterwards.
A short-lived ClickUp session token (only if you use the Clips tab) To save a recording to your Clips Hub, which ClickUp’s public API cannot do. The token is obtained from your existing ClickUp sign-in in this browser. Kept in memory while the panel is open, never written to disk. Sent only to ClickUp (id.app.clickup.com to obtain it, your workspace’s *.clickup.com API host to upload). The recording goes to the same ClickUp host.
Your last-used Workspace / Space / Folder / List To preselect them next time. Stored only in your browser.
The address (URL) of your current tab To detect when you are viewing a ClickUp task, so it can be prefilled, and to know whether the page can be captured. Read locally and never stored or sent anywhere. Only a detected task ID is sent to ClickUp, to look up that task.

Page access, screen, microphone and camera

  • The extension can capture the page you are viewing only when you click a screenshot button. “Area” mode briefly shows a selection overlay on the page, which is removed as soon as you finish. The extension does not otherwise read, collect or change the content of the websites you visit.
  • Screen recording and “Screen” screenshots start only when you click them, and your browser always shows its own picker so you choose exactly what is shared. Recording stops when you press Stop, use the browser’s “Stop sharing” control or, in Firefox, close the sidebar.
  • The microphone is used only during a recording you start with “Microphone” ticked.
  • The camera is used only during a recording you start with a camera selected (the default is “No camera”). Its picture is drawn into that recording as a small bubble; it is never stored or sent on its own.
  • If you turn on background blur for the camera bubble (off by default), each camera frame is analysed on your device to tell you apart from your background, using Google’s open-source MediaPipe library, which is packaged inside the extension. Frames and results never leave your browser. That library has built-in usage logging to Google; the extension blocks it, and the browser itself only lets the extension connect to ClickUp.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, receive or store your data on any server of ours.
  • We do not use analytics, telemetry, tracking pixels or advertising in the extension.
  • We do not sell, rent or share your data with anyone, and do not use it for any purpose other than the feature you are using.
  • We do not use your data to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes.
  • We do not load or run code from remote servers. Everything the extension runs is packaged inside it.
  • The optional “Buy me a coffee” link simply opens the Buy Me a Coffee website in a new tab. The extension sends no data to it; anything you do there is covered by that site’s own privacy policy.

This website

This website (clickup-uploader.macawshub.com) is separate from the extension. It counts visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which works without cookies and without fingerprinting: it records aggregate page views, referrers, countries and page-performance timings, and does not store IP addresses or any personal identifiers. It also uses Google Analytics, but only if you accept it in the cookie banner. Google Analytics sets cookies (_ga, _ga_*) to tell visits apart and records pages viewed, referrers, approximate location and device type; Google processes this data under its own privacy policy. You can change your choice at any time with "Cookie settings" at the bottom of the home page (declining deletes these cookies), or opt out with Google's browser add-on or by blocking cookies for this site. The extension itself loads nothing from this website and never reports anything to it.

The home page has a short tour video hosted on YouTube. Nothing is loaded from YouTube until you click "Watch the 55-second tour"; the player then comes from YouTube's privacy-enhanced domain (youtube-nocookie.com), and Google processes that playback under its own privacy policy.

How long data is kept

  • Recordings, screenshots and files: until you upload or remove them in the side panel. Unfinished screenshot drafts are deleted automatically after 24 hours.
  • Your API token and preferences: until you press Revoke in the extension’s settings or uninstall the extension.
  • Uninstalling the extension removes everything it stored in your browser.

Once uploaded, attachments and task details are stored by ClickUp and governed by ClickUp’s privacy policy. To revoke the extension’s access entirely, regenerate your API token in ClickUp → Settings → Apps.

Security

All communication with ClickUp uses HTTPS. Your token never leaves your browser except in requests to api.clickup.com. Anyone with access to your browser profile could read locally stored data, so use Revoke on shared computers.
